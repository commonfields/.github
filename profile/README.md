> **Private, local-first tools for indie builders.**

Commonfields builds open-source developer tools that keep your data on your
machine — no SaaS, no subscriptions, no telemetry. We make software for people
who ship: small, fast, native binaries that do one job well.

## What we believe

- **Private by default.** Your prompts and data never leave your machine.
- **Local-first.** Your own API keys, or 100% free with Ollama. No per-query pricing.
- **Open-source.** MIT-licensed. Your data stays in SQLite on your machine — no lock-in.
- **Honest software.** We measure and report probabilities, not guarantees.
